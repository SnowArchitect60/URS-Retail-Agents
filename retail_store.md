## 1. Business Overview

**Business name:** Mabuhay Home & Tech  
**Business type:** Philippine retail MSME  
**Location:** Quezon City, Metro Manila, Philippines  
**Established:** 2021  
**Primary channels:** Physical store, Facebook/Instagram messaging, and online orders through the store's website.

Mabuhay Home & Tech sells practical household electronics, small appliances, desk accessories, and selected personal-tech products. The store targets students, young professionals, families, and small home offices.

The store positions itself around straightforward product advice, reasonable pricing, and after-sales support rather than luxury or premium branding.

## 6. Delivery

The store delivers within Metro Manila and selected nearby areas.

- Metro Manila standard delivery: 1–3 business days
- Nearby provinces: usually 2–5 business days
- Same-day delivery may be available for selected Metro Manila orders placed before 1:00 PM.
- Delivery fees depend on destination, parcel size, and courier availability.
- The customer-service agent should not promise same-day delivery unless the customer confirms that the item and destination qualify.

## 7. Payment Methods

Orders are considered confirmed only after the required payment or order confirmation step has been completed.

## 8. Returns and Exchanges

Customers should contact the store within **7 days** of receiving an item if it arrives damaged, defective, incomplete, or materially different from the order.

For damage claims, customers may be asked to provide:

1. Order number
2. Photos of the package
3. Photos or video showing the issue
4. A brief description of what happened

Change-of-mind returns are evaluated case-by-case and may be subject to product condition and packaging requirements.

## 9. Warranty

Warranty coverage depends on the product and manufacturer.

The store can assist customers with warranty coordination, but the customer-service agent should not promise a manufacturer warranty period unless that period is explicitly available in the product record.

## 10. Customer-Service Principles

The store aims to:

- Give clear and honest product information.
- Avoid inventing stock availability.
- Explain delivery expectations conservatively.
- Escalate unresolved warranty or refund cases to a human staff member.
- Protect customer privacy.
- Avoid requesting unnecessary personal information.

## 12. External Price Comparison

The store sometimes compares its prices against publicly listed prices from other retailers.

For a live comparison, the price-comparison agent should:

1. Search for the same product/model first.
2. If unavailable, search for a genuinely comparable product.
3. Record the source and date/context of the listed price.
4. Separate item price from shipping, discounts, bundles, and membership pricing.
5. Avoid treating different specifications as equivalent.

## 13. Escalation Cases

The AI assistant should recommend human review when a customer:

- Disputes a payment.
- Requests an exception to a return policy.
- Reports a potentially unsafe product.
- Has a complicated warranty claim.
- Requests access to another person's account or order information.
- Provides insufficient information for a reliable answer.
